Undergraduate thesis · Mortality modeling

Forecasting Cause-Specific Mortality

I compared a long short-term memory model (LSTM) with a vector error correction model (VECM) to see how model choice changes long-term mortality projections for Sweden.

Historical data
1951–2024
Data preparation
19 age groups
Forecast horizon
2025–2035
Implementation
R and Python

The LSTM had lower reported error for cancer and for infectious and parasitic diseases in both sexes. The VECM had lower error for female respiratory mortality and male circulatory mortality. The two models also diverged in their projections for infectious and parasitic diseases, with the VECM projecting an increase and the LSTM projecting a flatter trend.

Explore methods and code
  1. Prepare the data in R. I harmonized ICD-7 through ICD-10 records from the WHO Mortality Database, aligned deaths and population counts by age and sex, and aggregated rates into five cause-specific series per sex. The five groups cover cancer, circulatory and respiratory diseases, infectious and parasitic diseases, and external causes.
  2. Fit and evaluate the models. I used Johansen cointegration tests and fitted the VECM with urca and vars. In Python, I used scikit-learn to standardize the training series and Keras to fit an LSTM on sliding windows of log mortality rates. The historical evaluation used 1951–2009 for training and 2010–2024 for evaluation. The LSTM tuning used rolling-window RMSE with observed inputs, while the VECM script produced a fixed-origin multi-step forecast. The reported errors reflect those different evaluation setups.
  3. Project and test sensitivity. I refit both models on data through 2024 for the 2025–2035 projections. In a separate LSTM sensitivity check, I compared projections from models trained through 2019 and 2024 over the shared 2025–2035 horizon to see how including the pandemic-era observations changed the projections.

Our team explored where and when parking tickets were issued in Vancouver from 2014 to 2025 to help drivers recognize common situations that lead to tickets.

Read project summary
